## English (default)

**Name:** Combined Invoice for AliExpress

**Short description (from the extension):** Combines selected AliExpress orders into one invoice whose total matches the bank payment.

**Description:**

```
Paid for several AliExpress orders in one checkout and need one invoice for your bookkeeping? Combined Invoice for AliExpress puts all orders behind one bank payment on one invoice, and its total matches the amount charged to your card to the cent.

HOW IT WORKS
• Open My orders on AliExpress. The extension adds a checkbox to every order and a small panel.
• Enter the amount from your bank statement and, optionally, the payment date. Click "Find orders by amount": the extension finds the orders from one day that add up exactly to that amount and loads older orders by itself when needed.
• Click "Create invoice". The invoice lists every order with its items, quantities and prices, shipping, discounts, fees and a VAT summary.
• Choose your company and print the invoice or save it as a PDF. The PDF has no browser header or footer, only page numbers.

WHY THE TOTALS MATCH
All amounts come from the official receipt of each order (the same data AliExpress shows under the "Receipt" button), not from guessing what is on the page. The extension handles summary lines that AliExpress shows twice, symbolic shipping fees that are not charged, and orders that AliExpress skips in the order list.

FEATURES
• Find orders by bank amount and payment date (also 1 to 3 days before it, because banks post card payments later)
• Add a missing order by its number from the confirmation e-mail
• Items, shipping and discounts as separate lines, or spread into item prices (handy for stock records)
• Several companies with company ID, tax ID and VAT ID
• Optional notes on the invoice for goods shipped from an EU warehouse and for shipping without tracking
• Interface and invoice in 17 languages: English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Polish, Czech, Slovak, Hungarian, Romanian, Ukrainian, Russian, Turkish, Japanese and Korean. The invoice language is set separately from the interface.
• Backup and restore of settings

PRIVACY
Everything runs in your browser. The extension reads orders only from your own signed-in AliExpress account, keeps settings only locally and sends no data anywhere. No account, no tracking, no ads. Source code: https://github.com/Dyonos/combined-invoice-for-aliexpress

This extension is not affiliated with AliExpress or Alibaba. The invoice is compiled by you from your AliExpress order data; check your local accounting rules for how to use it.
```

AliExpressで複数の注文を一度に支払い、経理処理用に請求書を1つにまとめたいとお考えですか？AliExpress用まとめ請求書は、銀行明細の1件の支払いに含まれるすべての注文を1つの請求書にまとめます。請求書の合計は、カードで支払った金額と端数まで正確に一致します。

【使い方】
• AliExpressで「私の注文」を開きます。拡張機能が各注文にチェックボックスを追加し、小さなパネルを表示します。
• 銀行明細の金額と、任意で支払日を入力します。「金額で注文を検索」をクリックすると、合計がちょうどその金額になる同じ日の注文が見つかります。必要に応じて、拡張機能が古い注文も自動で読み込みます。
• 「請求書を作成」をクリックします。請求書には、各注文の商品、数量、価格、送料、割引、手数料と、付加価値税の内訳が記載されます。

【合計が一致する理由】
すべての金額は、ページの表示から推測したものではなく、各注文の公式な領収書（AliExpressが注文に表示するのと同じデータ）から取得しています。AliExpressが二重に表示する合計行、実際には請求されない名目上の送料、AliExpressが注文一覧に表示しない注文にも対応しています。

• 一覧にない注文を、確認メールに記載の注文番号で追加
